Modules of Inquiry.

I.

Time Value of Money & Valuation Foundations

An exploration of the fundamental principle that a unit of currency today is worth more than the same unit tomorrow. This module establishes the mathematical bedrock for all subsequent financial analysis, covering present and future value, annuities, and perpetuities.

Key Concepts: DCF Analysis, Compounding, Yield Curves.

Deliverable: Valuation Model Assignment 1.

II.

Risk, Return, and the Cost of Capital

Investigating the immutable trade-off between risk and expected return. We will deconstruct the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM), examine historical market risk premiums, and calculate the Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC) as the hurdle rate for investment.

Key Concepts: Beta, Systematic Risk, WACC.

Deliverable: Cost of Equity Calculation Memo.

III.

Investment Decision Rules

A critical assessment of the methodologies corporations utilize to allocate scarce capital. The module contrasts Net Present Value (NPV) with Internal Rate of Return (IRR), highlighting the limitations and mathematical anomalies of IRR in mutually exclusive projects.

Key Concepts: NPV, IRR, Payback Period, Profitability Index.

Deliverable: Project Selection Case Study.

IV.

Real Options and Strategic Flexibility

Moving beyond static DCF analysis to value managerial flexibility. This advanced module introduces option pricing theory applied to real assets, exploring options to expand, abandon, or delay capital investments in uncertain environments.

Key Concepts: Black-Scholes Adaptation, Binomial Trees.

Deliverable: Final Real Options Analysis Paper.
