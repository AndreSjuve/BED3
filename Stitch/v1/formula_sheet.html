Key Formularies

A curated reference of fundamental equations for capital allocation and corporate valuation. Variables denoted in oxblood require period-specific inputs.

I. Net Present Value & Cash Flows

Net Present Value (NPV)

NPV = Σ CFt / (1 + r)t - C0
  • CFt : Cash flow in period t
  • r : Discount rate (cost of capital)
  • C0 : Initial investment

Internal Rate of Return (IRR)

0 = Σ CFt / (1 + IRR)t - C0
  • IRR : Rate where NPV equals zero
  • Requires iterative calculation or interpolation.

Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF)

FCFF = EBIT(1 - t) + D&A - ΔNWC - CapEx
  • EBIT : Earnings before interest and taxes
  • t : Corporate tax rate
  • D&A : Depreciation and amortization
  • ΔNWC : Change in net working capital

II. Cost of Capital Models

Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)

WACC = (E/V) × Re + (D/V) × Rd × (1 - t)
  • E : Market value of equity
  • D : Market value of debt
  • V : Total market value (E + D)
  • Re : Cost of equity
  • Rd : Cost of debt
  • t : Corporate tax rate

Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM)

Re = Rf + β × (Rm - Rf)
  • Rf : Risk-free rate
  • β : Levered Beta of the asset
  • (Rm - Rf) : Market risk premium

Unlevered Beta

βu = βL / [1 + (1 - t)(D/E)]
  • Removes capital structure effects to isolate operating risk.

III. Valuation Multiples

Enterprise Value (EV)

EV = Mkt Cap + Debt - Cash

EV / EBITDA

Multiple = EV / EBITDA

Capital structure neutral.

Price to Earnings (P/E)

Ratio = Price per Share / EPS

Impacted by leverage.

***

Reference Variables

  • Risk-Free Rate (Rf) Yield on 10-year US Treasury bonds
  • Market Risk Premium (MRP) Historical excess return of market over risk-free rate
  • Corporate Tax Rate (t) Effective marginal tax rate on corporate income

Key Concepts

Valuation models are highly sensitive to discount rate assumptions. A minor adjustment in the weighted average cost of capital can significantly alter the estimated enterprise value.

Ensure all period-specific variables remain consistent throughout multi-stage growth extrapolations.
