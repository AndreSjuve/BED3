Overview

Capital budgeting is the process a business uses to evaluate potential major projects or investments. Building a new plant or taking a large stake in an outside venture are examples of projects that typically require capital budgeting before they are approved or rejected.

This course provides a rigorous, quantitative foundation in corporate financial theory, focusing primarily on the valuation of real assets and the optimal financing of those investments. Students will master techniques such as Net Present Value (NPV), Internal Rate of Return (IRR), and real options analysis within the context of uncertainty and strategic market behavior.

Net Present Value (NPV) Profile

The NPV profile graphically illustrates the relationship between a project's net present value and the discount rate applied. The intersection with the horizontal axis represents the project's Internal Rate of Return (IRR).
