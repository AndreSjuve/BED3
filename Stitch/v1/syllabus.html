Syllabus & Course Objectives

A comprehensive exploration of corporate investment decisions, project valuation, and the strategic allocation of capital. This syllabus outlines the rigorous academic expectations and weekly progressions.

Course Modules

Week I

Foundations of Net Present Value (NPV)

Introduction to time value of money, discounting principles, and the foundational mathematics of capital budgeting. We establish the primary criterion for investment decisions.

  • Reading: Chapter 1 & 2
  • Assignment: Present Value Calculations
Week II

Risk and Return in Capital Markets

Historical perspectives on market risk, the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM), and estimating the cost of equity capital. Understanding the risk premium.

  • Reading: Chapter 4
  • Case Study: The Beta Anomaly
Week III

Advanced Valuation & Real Options

Beyond standard DCF analysis. Incorporating managerial flexibility into valuation models. Decision trees and basic real option valuation frameworks.

  • Reading: Chapter 7
  • Midterm Examination
Week IV

Capital Structure & Dividend Policy

The Modigliani-Miller theorems, taxes, costs of financial distress, and agency costs. How firms choose to finance their operations and distribute cash to shareholders.

  • Reading: Chapter 9 & 10
  • Final Project Presentations
